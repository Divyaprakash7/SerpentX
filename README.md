## How to play

1. Download `SerpentX.html`.
2. Open it in any modern browser.
3. Choose a level and press **Play now**.

Eat the apples to grow and raise your score. The game ends if you hit a wall, a rock or your own tail.

## Features

- **Three levels**
  - **Easy:** classic rules with solid walls and no rocks.
  - **Medium:** no walls, because the edges wrap around, plus 12 rocks.
  - **Hard:** solid walls, 14 rocks and a faster pace.
- **Speed slider:** from 1 to 10, adjustable during a game. The snake also speeds up a little with each apple.
- **Game controls:** Pause, Restart and Exit buttons.
- **Results screen:** shows your final score, level, best score, snake length and what ended the run.
- **Best score:** saved in your browser between sessions.
- **Start screen:** a demo snake plays in the background.
- **Drawn graphics:** a snake with a head, eyes and tongue, a shaded apple and stone-style rocks.
- **Light and dark:** follows your system theme.

## Controls

| Action | Keys |
|---|---|
| Steer | Arrow keys or WASD |
| Pause or resume | Space or P |
| Restart | R |
| Exit to the menu | Esc |
| Touch screens | Swipe |

## Tech

Plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript drawn on a `<canvas>`, with no libraries or build step.
